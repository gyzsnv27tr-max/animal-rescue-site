Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming animals in need across the Twin Cities area. Every animal deserves a loving home and a second chance.

Our Impact

Since our founding, we have rescued over 1,200 animals, placed more than 950 pets into permanent homes, and partnered with 40 local foster families.

How You Can Help

You can support our mission by adopting a pet, fostering an animal in need, volunteering your time, or spreading the word about our upcoming events.
